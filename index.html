County Food & Health Explorer

Text-to-SQL over the USDA Food Environment Atlas and County Health Rankings, cross-checked against live CDC PLACES. Every number traces to a tool call you can open.

What it explores

Where in a state do limited food access, poverty and food-assistance need coincide with diabetes, obesity and food insecurity, county by county, and does the latest CDC data still show the same places?

Who it is for

State and county health-department analysts deciding where nutrition and chronic-disease programs (SNAP-Ed, produce prescriptions, farmers'-market incentives) should go first.

How to use it

  • Name a state and the conditions you care about ("limited access above the state median and diabetes above the median").
  • Say which counties to leave out or keep in; the agent passes them as explicit parameters.
  • Follow up without repeating yourself: the session remembers the state and the selected counties.
  • Ask "does the latest CDC data agree?" to pull PLACES estimates for the selected counties.
